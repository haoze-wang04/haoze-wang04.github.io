I received my B.S. in Computer Science (summa cum laude) from Peking University, where I was a member of the Turing Class.

I am currently a **Visiting Scholar (Research Fellowship)** at the Max Planck Institute for Informatics, hosted by Prof. Danupon Na Nongkai (November 2026–January 2027).

My research interests lie in theoretical computer science, particularly **graph algorithms, dynamic algorithms, streaming algorithms, and approximation algorithms**.

I have worked with the TCS groups at Peking University and the University of Warwick, under the guidance of Shaofeng Jiang and Sayan Bhattacharya. My research explores fundamental algorithmic problems, including Max-Cut, spanning trees, shortest paths, and connectivity under failures.

My papers have been accepted to STOC 2026, ISAAC 2026, and SOSA 2027.

Besides research, I am interested in games and game design, as well as novels and creative writing.
