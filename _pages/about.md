I received my B.S. in Computer Science (summa cum laude) from Peking University, where I was a member of the Turing Class.

My research interests lie in theoretical computer science, particularly in the design and analysis of algorithms, with a focus on graph algorithms, approximation algorithms, and streaming algorithms.

I have worked with the TCS groups at Peking University and the University of Warwick, under the guidance of Shaofeng Jiang and Sayan Bhattacharya. My research explores fundamental algorithmic problems, including Max-Cut, spanning trees, and connectivity under failures.

My work has been published to venues including STOC and ISAAC.

Beside research, I am also interested in games and designing games, and, novels and writing novels.

I will start an internship at MPI soon.

**I am currently seeking Ph.D. positions.**
